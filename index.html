About Us

A Local Cleaning Team You Can Leave To It

We are based in Ledbury and clean across Herefordshire and the Malverns. Some customers have us in every week, some call us once a year for a proper going over before guests arrive or a tenancy ends. Either way you get the same team, the same standard, and a job that is finished properly rather than rushed.

You get a clear price for the clean before we book it in, and we tell you honestly how long the job will take.
